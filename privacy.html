Hermes Workspace

Privacy policy

Hermes Workspace is a personal integration operated by Minta Wood. It uses Google OAuth to access Drive file metadata and the content of files, Docs, and Sheets needed for tasks the account owner requests. It can also write to those services when directed by the owner.

How data is used

Hermes uses Google data to fulfill the owner's requests. When a task requires AI processing, relevant excerpts or tool results may be sent to the configured model provider, currently OpenAI through the owner's ChatGPT connection. Google data is used for the requested task and is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and control

Hermes stores the OAuth credential in the owner's local Hermes configuration. Retrieved information may be retained in local conversation history, output files, and backups until the owner removes those records. The owner can remove local copies and revoke the connection from their Google Account.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
